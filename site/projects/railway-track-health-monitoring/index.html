Smart Railway Track Health Monitoring

ESP32-Based IoT Track Safety & Signaling Prototype

ROLE / CONTRIBUTION Embedded Firmware & Circuit Prototyping
INSTITUTION PSTU CSE Department
View Repository

Verified Technologies Used

C/C++ (Arduino/ESP-IDF) ESP32 Ultrasonic Sensors OLED Display Buzzer Alert Module Hardware Prototyping
Problem Being Solved

The Challenge

Railway track defects, unexpected track obstructions, and misaligned signaling cause severe derailment hazards that require real-time sensing and rapid alert delivery.

Engineering Solution

The Approach

A hardware and firmware prototype powered by an ESP32 microcontroller that continuously samples ultrasonic obstacle sensors, calculates oncoming speed via transit time, triggers automatic signals, and displays metrics on a local OLED screen and buzzer alert module.

Architecture & Decisions

System Architecture

Event loop architecture running on the ESP32. Non-blocking timing prevents sensor polling delays and keeps the OLED display refreshed and emergency buzzer responsive.

Implemented debounced sensor readings to filter false positive obstacle alarms caused by environmental vibrations.

Contribution & Scope

My Work & Current Limitations

Programmed the ESP32 firmware in C/C++, calibrated distance sensor thresholds, implemented speed estimation mathematics, and wired the breadboard circuit prototype with signaling LEDs and audio alarms.

Current Scope: Academic bench prototype. Not certified or deployed on commercial or actual operational railway infrastructure.
